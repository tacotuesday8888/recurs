03 / OPEN SOURCE

Visible roles.
Bounded authority.
Your call.

Recurs is public alpha software for macOS and Linux. Company runs are inspectable, candidate work stays staged until policy permits application, and failed work is reported as failed.

No magic score. No claim that more agents always win. Just a controllable harness, versioned evidence, and an Apache-2.0 repository you can inspect.
